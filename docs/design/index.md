# Developer & Specifications Archive

This archive preserves the engineering scoping census, dependency DAG analysis, stage-by-stage technical specifications, and historical per-stage benchmark reports generated during the migration of upstream Fortran MESA (`r24.08.1`) into `FlexMESA`'s Three-Tier Hybrid Architecture (`Safe Rust` + `Python/PyO3` + `JAX`).

For user-facing domain physics, governing equations, and usage examples, see the **[Background & Physics](../concepts/architecture.md)** section. For the unified synthesis of four-way numerical parity and runtime performance comparisons against compiled upstream Fortran MESA static archives (`lib*.a`), see the **[Verification & Benchmarks Overview](../benchmarks/overview.md)**, **[Kernel Performance Scorecards](../benchmarks/kernel-scorecards.md)**, and **[Tolerance & Discrepancy Ledger](../benchmarks/tolerance-and-discrepancy-ledger.md)**.

## Scoping & Architectural Specifications

- **[Master Scoping & Architecture](scoping-and-architecture.md)**: Line-of-code census of upstream `MESAHub/mesa`, the 12-tier acyclic module dependency DAG, the Index & Memory-Order Contract (`1..nz` column-major Fortran vs. `0..nz-1` C-contiguous row-major `FlexMESA`), and explicit scope boundaries (`PGSTAR` $\to$ `flexmesa.plotting`, pulsation export boundary for `GYRE`/`FGONG`/`OSC`, and unified `TDC`/`RSP2`).
- **[Wave 0 Infrastructure Specification](wave-0-spec.md)**: Declarative `.defaults` and Fortran `inlist` schema validation (`flexmesa.schema`), deterministic `.safetensors` physics table encoding and `SHA-256` manifest verification (`flexmesa.data`), and upstream `<pkg>/test/test_output` golden baseline comparators (`flexmesa.testing`).
- **[Wave 1 Foundation & Microphysics Specification](wave-1-spec.md)**: Physical constants (`const`), utilities (`utils`), IEEE-754 elementary intrinsics (`math`), const-generic forward-mode dual and hyperdual numbers (`auto_diff`), 1D monotonic and 2D bicubic splines (`interp_1d`, `interp_2d`), dense/banded/block-tridiagonal linear solvers (`mtx`), root-finders and stiff ODE integrators (`num`), 5-channel thermal neutrinos (`neu`), and local/time-dependent turbulent convection (`turb`).
- **[Wave 1.5 Hybrid Consolidation Specification](wave-1-5-spec.md)**: Unification of the Three-Tier Hybrid Architecture (`Tier A` canonical Safe Rust, `Tier B` dual Safe Rust + `@jax.jit` microphysics, `Tier C` Hirshman `bcyclic` + GPU Parallel Cyclic Reduction + `@jax.custom_vjp`), zero-copy `PyO3` bindings, and the 4-way Fortran MESA benchmark harness.
- **[Wave 2 Atomic & Photometric Specification](wave-2-spec.md)**: 347 built-in reference nuclides (plus full `isotopes.data` parser), 286 solar isotopes, and 10 solar abundance scales (`AG89` through `AAG21`) (`chem`), partial ionization and effective ionic charges (`ionization`), and 32-gather 3D cubic Hermite Kurucz SED synthesis and non-uniform Simpson filter photometry (`colors`).
- **[Wave 3a Analytical & Single-Table EOS Specification](wave-3a-spec.md)**: Canonical 26-slot thermodynamic state vector, Aparicio (1998) Gauss-Fermi integrals, neutral `Ideal` gas, Timmes & Swesty (2000) biquintic/bicubic `HELM`, Potekhin & Chabrier (2010) `PC`, Jermyn et al. (2021) third-order `Skye` crystallization EOS, $C^2$ polygonal quintic blending (`eos_dt_get`), and $(P_{\mathrm{gas}}, T)$ root-finding inversions (`eos_pt_get`).
- **[Wave 3b 4D Tabulated EOS, Opacity, Rates, Networks & Atmospheres Specification](wave-3b-spec.md)**: 4D $(X, Z, \log_{10} Q, \log_{10} T)$ tabulated equations of state (`OPAL/SCVH`, `FreeEOS`, `CMS`), radiative/conductive/Compton opacities (`kap`), 316 REACLIB rates and plasma screening (`rates`), SIMD dual-number reaction networks and stiff 1-zone burners (`net`), and grey/irradiated/tabulated model atmospheres (`atm`).
- **[Wave 4 1D Stellar Structure & Evolution Specification](wave-4-spec.md)**: Staggered 1D Lagrangian stellar structure (`star_data`, `star`), 8-mass `ZAMS` catalog, 3-cell stencil automatic differentiation (`StarAdOrder1`), coupled Henyey Newton-Raphson solver, overshooting, stellar winds, adaptive remeshing (`mesh_adjust`), and Söderlind-Wang H211 timestep control (`varcontrol`).
- **[Wave 5 Advanced Stellar Physics, Interacting Binaries & Asteroseismology Specification](wave-5-spec.md)**: Burgers multi-fluid atomic diffusion, Caplan OCP strong coupling, crystallization phase separation, Roche shellular rotation and Spruit-Tayler dynamo, well-balanced 1D spherical HLLC Riemann hydrodynamics, `RSP2` radial pulsation, `GYRE`/`FGONG`/`OSC` exporters, coupled interacting binary evolution (`binary`), and asteroseismic scaling, surface corrections, and optimization (`astero`).
