## Migration Wave & Phase `X.5` Roadmap

Every primary wave (`Wave X`) is followed by a mandatory **`Wave X.5` (`Holistic Review, Optimization, Upstream Fortran MESA Gate & Public Docs Sync`)** stage. Progression to the next wave requires passing both the `FlexMESA` CI suite and all compare-to-original-Fortran-MESA parity and performance benchmarks in `benchmarks/fortran_mesa/`.
