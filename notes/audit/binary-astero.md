## 1a. Step driver, implicit mass transfer, retry/redo

### F3-1: The coupled step is not upstream's part1 / adjust_mdots / part2 sequence; each star runs a complete `star_evolve_step` (with its own internal retries) one after the other
- Area: flexmesa-binary/system_step.rs, system_rlof.rs
- Severity: wrong numerics (structure)
- FlexMESA: crates/flexmesa-binary/src/system_step.rs:284-300 (`prepare_coupled_mdots_with_extras(...)?; let dt_used = evolve_single_star_without_term_rewind(&mut self.donor, dt_try, allow_remesh)?.dt; ... let rep_a = evolve_single_star_without_term_rewind(s2, dt_used, allow_remesh)?; if rep_a.dt < 0.999_999 * dt_used { return Ok(SingleTrialOutcome::CutDt(rep_a.dt)); }`)
- Upstream: binary/private/run_binary_support.f90:430-458 (`star_evolve_step_part1(id, first_try)` for donor then accretor), :461 (`call adjust_mdots(b)`), :481-505 (`star_evolve_step_part2` for both), :510-520 (`binary_evolve_step`, `binary_check_model`), :542-544 (`binary_pick_next_timestep`), :587-615 (`star_prepare_to_redo` / `star_prepare_to_retry` for both stars + binary)
- Confidence: verified-by-reading-both-sides
- What: Upstream computes each star's wind in `part1` (which includes the full `do_set_mdot` wind logic and `extras_start_step` context), then `adjust_mdots` edits `s% mstar_dot` in place, then `part2` solves. A retry of either star retries both stars and the binary at a common reduced dt via the star retry machinery. FlexMESA instead pre-computes winds by a side call (`do_set_mdot` with `mass_change=0`, system_rlof.rs:258-272), forces `mass_change` + `use_other_adjust_mdot`, then runs a whole `star_evolve_step` on the donor (which may internally retry and shorten dt), then the accretor at the donor's `dt_used`. The donor's internal retries are invisible to the binary (they change dt after the mass-transfer accounting was fixed for `dt_try`), and an accretor cut restarts the whole trial from a clone. Order of physics is the same in outline (winds, mdots, donor then accretor, orbit, check), but the retry topology and where the wind is evaluated are FlexMESA constructions.
- Proposed fix: split `star_evolve_step` into the upstream part1/part2 pair and drive both stars from the binary loop with shared `retry`/`redo` results; L.

### F3-2: Orbital angular momentum is integrated with an 8-substep RK2 midpoint scheme; upstream takes one explicit Euler step
- Area: flexmesa-binary/system_step.rs
- Severity: wrong numerics (non-upstream integrator; flatters the analytic oracles)
- FlexMESA: crates/flexmesa-binary/src/system_step.rs:371-372 (`let n_sub = 8_usize; let h = dt_used / (n_sub as f64);`), :398 (`let j_m = j_cur + 0.5 * jb0.jdot_total * h;`), :418 (`j_cur += jb_m.jdot_total * h;`)
- Upstream: binary/private/binary_evolve.f90:320-321 (`b% jdot = get_jdot(b)` / `b% angular_momentum_j = b% angular_momentum_j + b% jdot*b% time_step*secyer`)
- Confidence: verified-by-reading-both-sides
- What: Upstream evaluates jdot once, with end-of-step masses and start-of-step separation/period/J, and advances J by `jdot*dt`. FlexMESA re-evaluates jdot 16 times per step along linearly interpolated masses with self-consistent a(J), P(J). This is second order where upstream is first order, so `jdot_gr_check` (1e-2) and `jdot_ml_check` (1e-4) are compared against the analytic solutions with a more accurate integrator than the code being reproduced; passing those oracles says nothing about parity with upstream's trajectory, and the timestep controller (`fj`, `fa`) sees different per-step changes. `b% jdot` is reported as the step average (:427) rather than the instantaneous value.
- Proposed fix: default to the upstream single Euler update (keep RK2 behind an explicit non-default option recorded in DECISIONS); S.

### F3-6: Tidal synchronisation is applied as a post-step relaxation of `j_rot`, not as `extra_jdot` inside the angular-momentum solve; spin is globally rescaled after each stellar step
- Area: flexmesa-binary/system_jdot.rs, system_step.rs
- Severity: wrong numerics
- FlexMESA: crates/flexmesa-binary/src/system_step.rs:336-358 (`apply_tidal_spin_sync(&mut self.donor, ...)` after both `star_evolve_step` calls); system_jdot.rs:259 (`star.j_rot[k] -= relax_frac * (star.j_rot[k] - sync_ratio * j_sync)`); system_jdot.rs:165-197 `conserve_spin_after_henyey` (`let scale = s_target / s_cur; ... star.omega[k] *= scale;`), called at system_step.rs:317
- Upstream: binary/private/binary_tides.f90:265-269 (`if (.not. b% doing_first_model_of_run) then ... s% extra_jdot(k) = s% extra_jdot(k) - delta_j(k)/dt_next`), i.e. a per-zone torque consumed by the star's own omega/j transport solve; binary_jdot.f90:152-157 (`delta_J = b% s_donor% total_angular_momentum_old - b% s_donor% total_angular_momentum; ... delta_J - b% s_donor% angular_momentum_removed * abs(b% mdot_system_wind(b% d_i) / b% s_donor% mstar_dot)`)
- Confidence: verified-by-reading-both-sides
- What: The per-zone `delta_j` formulas match upstream (Uniform: `(1-exp(-a2 dt/t_sync))(j - a1/a2 j_sync)`; tdyn_div_tkh iteration; `t_sync` from Hut eq. 11 with `k/T`; see Matches). The coupling does not. Upstream hands the torque to the star, which transports it together with rotational mixing in the same solve, using start-of-step `i_rot`/`j_rot`, and skips it on the first model. FlexMESA lets the star step without torque, then (a) when the star is not losing mass multiplies every zone's omega by a single factor so total spin equals the pre-step value plus an accretion estimate (`conserve_spin_after_henyey`), which implies the star crate's own step does not conserve J and the binary crate papers over it with a non-physical uniform rescale, and (b) applies the tidal relaxation on post-step `i_rot`, including on the first model. `jdot_ls` is then built from before/after spins of step (b) only, with the wind term estimated as `mdot * j_rot(surface, pre-step) * dt` (system_step.rs:315, jdot_tides.rs `eval_jdot_ls_with_winds`) instead of upstream's `angular_momentum_removed` accounted from the removed layers. `jdot_ls_check`'s 1e-12 "conservation" criterion (a FlexMESA addition, see T2-9) is satisfied by construction because `cumulative_j_ext` and `jdot_ls` are computed from the same differences.
- Proposed fix: implement `extra_jdot` in the star's omega transport and conserve J there; delete `conserve_spin_after_henyey`; compute `jdot_ls` from `total_angular_momentum_old - total_angular_momentum` and a real `angular_momentum_removed`; L.

### F3-7: Full `StarModel` deep copies are used for every redo/retry; cost and a cheaper mechanism
- Area: flexmesa-binary/system_step.rs, system_rlof.rs
- Severity: maintainability (performance) with a correctness side effect (counters)
- FlexMESA: crates/flexmesa-binary/src/system_step.rs:74 (`let pre_step_snap = self.capture_snapshot();` every step, explicit or implicit), :148 (`let mut trial_snap = self.capture_snapshot();` every outer attempt in implicit mode), :155, :199, :213 (`self.restore_snapshot(&trial_snap)?`), :95; system_rlof.rs:135 (`donor_state: donor.clone()`), :166-170 (`let d_ctrl = donor.controls.clone(); let d_job = donor.job.clone(); *donor = self.donor_state.clone(); donor.controls = d_ctrl; donor.job = d_job;`)
- Upstream: binary/private/run_binary_support.f90:587-615 (`star_prepare_to_redo(id)` / `star_prepare_to_retry(id)`), binary_evolve.f90:590-641 (`binary_set_current_to_old`)
- Confidence: verified-by-reading-flexmesa-only for the counts (struct fields counted by script from crates/flexmesa-star/src/model.rs and hydro_state.rs); verified-by-reading-both-sides for the mechanism
- What: What is cloned: `StarModel` has 154 fields, 106 `Vec<f64>`, 18 other `Vec`s (10 of them `Vec<StarAdOrder1>` = 25 f64 per zone, 3 `Vec<[f64; 26]>` EOS blocks), plus `StarControls`, `StarJobControls`, `EosConfig`, `KapConfig`; the nested `HydroAuxState` has 71 fields, 50 `Vec<f64>`, 6 more `Vec<StarAdOrder1>` and a `Vec<[f64; 24]>`. Per zone that is roughly 156 + 16x25 + 3x26 + 24 = about 660 f64 plus `xa` (species per zone), i.e. about 5.5 kB per zone, about 11 MB per 2000-zone star, about 160 heap allocations per star per clone, and all derived quantities (EOS results, AD partials, mixing arrays) that the next `set_hydro_vars` recomputes anyway. How many: a step that converges on try N of the implicit loop with no outer retry performs 2 captures + (N-1) restores, and each restore is itself a fresh `clone()` of the snapshot (system_rlof.rs:168, 177) plus two controls/job clones, so N+1 deep copies per star per step (2N+2 for two stars); each outer retry adds 2 more per star; a detachment adds 1; an explicit-mode step always pays 1. double_bh's `max_tries_to_achieve = 400` bounds this at about 800 star copies in one step.
  Upstream keeps one "old" generation of the primary state only (`xh_old`, `xa_old`, `q_old`, `dq_old`, `omega_old`/`j_rot_old`, a few dozen scalars) and `star_prepare_to_redo` swaps back to it; everything else is recomputed. Side effect in FlexMESA: because the entire struct is rolled back, `num_retries`, `total_num_solver_iterations` and the step-history counters accumulated by the star's internal retries during a discarded trial are erased (only `model_number_for_last_retry` and `timestep_hold` are carried across by `record_retry_holds`/`apply_retry_holds`, system_step.rs:555-585), and binary-level retries are never counted anywhere, so a run reports 0 retries per star regardless of how many trials were discarded.
- Proposed fix: add `StarModel::save_old_generation()` / `restore_old_generation()` that copy only primary variables (`xh`, `xa`, `dq`/`q`, `m`, `omega`/`j_rot`, `mstar`, `dt`, ages, step-history scalars) into preallocated buffers (`clone_from`/`copy_from_slice`, no allocation), then call `set_hydro_vars`; keep one binary-level old generation instead of two snapshots; exclude counters from rollback and add `num_binary_retries`/`num_binary_redos`. As an immediate S-size win, `restore_stars` can `clone_from` into the live model and `mem::swap` controls instead of cloning them; M overall.

  1. It only bites for dt below about 100 s. double_bh runs at dt of years to kyr; reaching 111 s means the step size has already collapsed by 8 or more orders of magnitude through retries. The "ceiling" is where a retry cascade ends, not why it began.
  2. The same reasoning applied to the earlier gold2 stages contradicts ordinary upstream behaviour. `gold2_tol_max_residual1 = 1d-9` (iterations 1-4) and `gold2_tol_max_residual2 = 1d-8` (iterations 5-9) (controls.defaults:9257, 9260) would give "ceilings" at 1.1e6 s and 1.1e5 s. If a one-ULP neighbour difference at the sigma limit were generic, no gold2 run could converge in fewer than 10 iterations at dt < 1.3 days, which is not what MESA does. So such differences are not generic upstream.
  3. A one-ULP difference between neighbours is removable by the Newton iteration in upstream's formulation. The unknown is `solver_dx` (magnitude of the per-step change, so its own ULP is many orders smaller), and `xa = xa_start + solver_dx` (solver_support.f90:925). Two neighbours with nearly equal true sums round to the same double except when they straddle a rounding boundary, so the converged state normally has exactly zero difference across fully mixed faces and the residual there is the (tiny) burning imbalance, not `sig/dm * ULP`. A persistent one-ULP pattern needs a source of per-zone rounding noise applied after the update: unconditional renormalisation by `sum_xa` (which A5 says FlexMESA used to do), storing `xa` and differencing to get `dxdt_actual` (the `valid_dx` fallback above), or clipping. Upstream avoids the first two by construction.
  4. "Identical floor" is therefore true only as a statement about a formula (given a one-ULP difference at a limited face, upstream's residual would be the same number). It is not evidence that upstream MESA would also fail there, and it is not a property upstream is known to hit in this case.
- Confidence: verified-by-reading-both-sides for all quoted lines; points 2-3 are inference from the code, not from a run.
