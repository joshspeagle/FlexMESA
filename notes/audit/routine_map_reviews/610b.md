# Independent review of the #610b GYRE and ADIPLS routine maps

- **Proposal reviewed**: branch `610b-gyre-adipls-proposed-maps` at `459dd125` (base `train-16` at `07f2cc52`). It adds `benchmarks/dashboard/routine_map_gyre_adipls_proposed.toml`, which maps routines of 104 upstream files (63 GYRE, 41 ADIPLS) that `port_manifest.toml` lists as `absent`.
- **Purpose**: decide whether these maps can evidence raising the 104 files from `absent` to `partial` (`DECISIONS #79`).
- **Upstream**: MESA `26.4.1` (`gyre/gyre/src/**/*.fypp`, `adipls/adipack.c/**`).
- **Reviewer**: independent review agent, 2026-11-27. Machine record: `610b.toml` in this directory.

## Verdict

The maps support the raise. In a stratified sample of 60 mappings, no Rust target implements a different routine than the one it names, and none names code that does not exist: 0 of 60 were `wrong-target` or `not-ported`. By the rule of three, the 95% upper bound on that rate is about 5%. The errors found change only whether a routine is graded full or partial; none of them makes a file-level `partial` claim false. Before the files are raised, the corrections under "Corrections before the raise" must be applied to the proposal.

## Frame

- Mapped routines: 330, out of the 529 routines in the 104 files.
  - Full: GYRE 211 (8,678 lines), ADIPLS 38 (5,752 lines).
  - Partial: GYRE 72 (4,916 lines), ADIPLS 9 (3,861 lines).
  - No routine is proposed as not applicable.
- Rust files named: 28 (7 in `flexmesa-adipls`, 21 in `flexmesa-gyre`).
- Mechanical check: every routine key named exactly one routine of `routine_inventory.json`, and every target symbol existed as a function item in its file. This was checked with a reimplementation of `routine_gate`'s two checks. The gate itself runs on each file when it is raised.

## Method

- **Sample**: 40 mappings claimed full and 20 claimed partial, seed `610204`.
  - Stratified by module (GYRE, ADIPLS) in proportion to the number of mappings, with at least 10 full and 6 partial per module.
  - Within each stratum, drawn without replacement with probability proportional to the square root of the routine's line count (Efraimidis-Spirakis keys `u^(1/w)` from Python's `random.Random(610204)`, mappings ordered by `(upstream_path, routine)`).
  - Result: GYRE 30 full and 14 partial, ADIPLS 10 full and 6 partial. That is 3,281 full lines and 4,398 partial lines, including the 2,094-line `adipls` driver.
- **Reading**: both sides were read for every sampled mapping: the upstream routine over its inventory line range, and every Rust function named in the target. For the `adipls` driver, the upstream option paths were enumerated and each was checked for a Rust counterpart.
- **Grades for mappings claimed full**:
  - `correct`: every branch, output, state write and error path is present, apart from terminal output and differences the language forces.
  - `over-claim`: something is missing, so the mapping should be partial.
  - `wrong-target`: the named code implements a different routine.
  - `not-ported`: no counterpart exists.
- **Grades for mappings claimed partial**: `correct`, `under-claim` (the port is in fact complete), `wrong-target` or `not-ported`.
- **Departures**: additions with no upstream counterpart (guards, clamps, fallbacks, reordered arithmetic) were recorded separately from the grade.
- **Who read the sample**: four reviewer agents. The lead reviewer re-read the worst findings (items F5, F13 and P14).

## Findings

1. **F36 `res_adipar` (`adipls/adipack.c/adipls/res_adipar.c.d.f:39-89`), claimed full: over-claim.** Upstream stores 12 reals and 9 integers on one call and restores them on the next. No Rust function does this. `crates/flexmesa-adipls/src/driver.rs` copies the controls from an immutable control state, and `crates/flexmesa-adipls/src/controls.rs` stores 14 fields. The restore of `eltrw1`, `eltrw2` and `irotkr` is missing. The store at `adipls.c.d.f:1333`, which keeps the `itrsig`, `nsel` and `itsord` resets, has no counterpart. It should be mapped partial or unmapped.
2. **F5 `dH_` (`gyre/gyre/src/rad/rad_trans_m.fypp:521-543`), claimed full: over-claim.** The mapped Rust (`crates/flexmesa-gyre/src/rad.rs`) implements the `JCD_SET` case only. The `LAGP_SET` branch and the invalid-set abort are absent, and the LAGP variable set is rejected when the transform is built. It should be mapped partial, with `dH_jcd_` as its own full entry.
3. **F13 `trans_cond` (`gyre/gyre/src/ad/ad_trans_m.fypp:241-287`), claimed full.** The `from = .FALSE.` branch is absent from `crates/flexmesa-gyre/src/ad.rs`. No GYRE caller passes `from = .FALSE.` to `trans_cond`; only `trans_vars` is called that way (`gyre/gyre/src/rad/rad_bvp_m.fypp:173`). The branch is therefore dead. Either mark the mapping partial, or keep it full with a dead-branch note.
4. **P3 `dW_dx` and P13 `integral_${S}$_`, claimed partial: arguable under-claims.**
   - P3: the only branch not ported is the `select type` arm for model types FlexMESA does not have.
   - P13: only the complex template instance is missing.
   - A single rule for templated `${S}$` routines and type-dispatch arms decides these and the similar items P0, P6 and P11.
5. **P14 `adipls` driver (`adipls/adipack.c/adipls/adipls.c.d.f:1-2094`), claimed partial: correct, with fidelity defects.**
   - `imdmod` is parsed but `modmod` is not called (`adipls.c.d.f:1613-1618`). There is no numerical effect, because the `modmod` MESA links is empty.
   - On `signew` returning `icry = -1`, the Rust leaves the `jstsig` loop, where upstream continues at label 75 (`adipls.c.d.f:1836-1840,1959-1962`).
   - Model truncation (`trnmod`) and several output paths are rejected as unsupported, as the partial level states.
6. **Departures found on mappings graded correct.** These are listed for the parity work, not for the levels.
   - `takata` skips upstream's reads of unset `yy` points: a `continue` when `k + iasn` passes `nn`, and zeros for points never filled. Both apply only when `iasn >= 2`.
   - `signew` returns its input `sigp` on `icry = -1`, where upstream leaves the result unassigned. Under `DECISIONS #43`, both of these should become explicit errors, each with a deferred issue.
   - `stevft` has extra guards and a `max(1)` clamp.
   - `forum.rs::unique_indices` adds an empty-input guard.
   - `search.rs` drops bad scan indices silently.
   - `alpha_grv /= 1` is a hard error where upstream warns.

## Corrections before the raise

- Mark `res_adipar` partial, or unmap it.
- Mark `dH_` partial.
- Decide `trans_cond`: partial, or full with a dead-branch note.
- Settle the rule for templated instances and type-dispatch arms.
- Re-read the full entries of the dispatch routines in the `*_trans_m`, `*_diff_m` and `*_bound_m` modules and the ADIPLS routines that save state between calls. The projected over-claims are concentrated there.

The defects in finding 5 and the `DECISIONS #43` cases in finding 6 are routed to issues. They do not block the raise.

## Not verified

- No GYRE, ADIPLS or MESA output was compared.
- No tests were run.
- The grades come from reading the source. A level judged correct here means the code is present, not that its numbers match MESA.

## Addendum: round 4 checked before the raise

Lane E's round 4 (`610b-gyre-adipls-proposed-maps` at `bb064255`) was checked against the corrections above before the conversion:

- `res_adipar` is unmapped. Its file, `adipls/adipack.c/adipls/res_adipar.c.d.f`, maps no other routine, so it stays `absent` and the proposal covers 103 files.
- `dH_` and `trans_cond` are partial in both `rad_trans_m` and `ad_trans_m`, together with `G_`, `H_`, `trans_eqns` and `trans_vars`, which share the `JCD_SET`-only dispatch or the one-direction `from` branch. `G_jcd_`, `H_jcd_`, `dH_jcd_` and `stencil` stay full.
- Templated and type-dispatch routines follow one rule: a routine is full only when every `${S}$` instance and every `select type` arm is ported. This keeps `dW_dx` and `integral_${S}$_` partial and marks `prox_search_2_` partial (no `tnad_bvp_t` arm).
- `nscfil` is partial: it checks the fixed ADIPLS output units rather than the general `common/cofile/` table.

Ten entries were re-read against upstream:
- `rad_trans_m::G_`, `rad_trans_m::dH_`, `ad_trans_m::H_` and `ad_trans_m::trans_eqns`: the `JCD_SET`/`LAGP_SET` dispatch, with LAGP absent in the port.
- `ad_trans_m::trans_vars` and `ad_trans_m::trans_cond`: only `from = .TRUE.` is ported.
- `prox_search_m::prox_search_2_`: the `select type` arm for `tnad_bvp_t` is absent.
- `ofiles::nscfil`: the port does not look up `common/cofile/` through `stfile`.
- `ad_diff_m::ad_diff_t_` stays full: its body only chooses between the difference-equation factory and the match conditions, and the unported schemes sit in `diff_factory_m`, which is partial.
- The removed `res_adipar` mapping.

All ten are graded correctly. The 103 files are raised from `absent` to `partial` under `DECISIONS #79` and are listed in `610b.toml`.
